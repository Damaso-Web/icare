## 1. What is iCARE

iCARE brings the work of the **Office of Student Services** into one place. It
connects three units:

- **Guidance and Counseling Unit (GCU)** — referrals, counseling cases, session notes.
- **Testing and Materials Development Unit (TMDU)** — psychological testing schedules and results.
- **Student Discipline Unit (SDU)** — complaints and incident reports.

A student is **referred** (by a teacher, dean, the student themselves, or OSS
staff). The referral becomes a **case**, the student is given **appointments**,
sessions are recorded, and — when needed — the student is sent for **testing** or
a **disciplinary** process. Everything about a student stays together.

Who uses it:

- **Students** — sign in with their Student ID to follow their referrals and appointments.
- **Faculty and Deans** — refer students and see what happened with their referrals.
- **OSS staff** — manage referrals, appointments, cases, testing and records.

## 2. Getting Started (everyone)

### 2.1 Opening iCARE

Open the iCARE website in a browser (Chrome, Edge or Firefox). You will land on
the public home page. Click **Log in** (or **Log in to iCARE**).

### 2.2 Choosing how to sign in

On the sign-in screen, pick the card that matches you:

- **BSU Personnel** — for OSS staff, faculty, deans and department chairs. You
  will then choose **Faculty** or **OSS Personnel**.
- **Student** — for students.

### 2.3 Signing in

- **Staff / Faculty:** enter your **email** and **password**.
- **Student:** enter your **Student ID** and **password**.

Tick **Remember me** to stay signed in on your own device. Use the **eye icon**
to show or hide your password.

> **First login:** depending on how your account was set up, you may be asked to
> **set a new password** the first time. Choose a strong password you will
> remember.

### 2.4 Forgot your password

Click **Forgot password?**, enter your email (staff) or the email on your student
account, and follow the link sent to your inbox. If you do not see it, check your
**spam folder**, and use **Resend email** if needed (there is a short wait between
sends). The reset link expires after a few minutes.

### 2.5 Students: first-time registration

New students click **Register with your Student ID** on the Student sign-in page,
enter their Student ID and details, and set a password. On first sign-in you must
read and accept the **Data Privacy Notice and Consent Authorization** before you
can continue.

### 2.6 Signing out

Use the **Sign out** button (in the menu / sidebar). On a shared computer, always
sign out when you finish.

## 3. Things every staff account shares

No matter your role, these are always available once you sign in:

- **Dashboard** — your home screen. It shows the numbers and lists that matter to
  your role (for example, new referrals, open cases, upcoming appointments). The
  dashboard and the main lists **refresh on their own**, so new items appear
  without reloading the page.
- **Notifications** — the **bell icon** shows new activity (a referral assigned to
  you, an appointment confirmed, a case update). A red badge means unread items.
  Open **View all** to see the full list.
- **My Account** — update your name, contact number and password. Changing your
  email asks for your current password for safety.
- **Search and filters** — list pages (Referrals, Cases, etc.) have a search box
  and filters (status, type, date range) to find records quickly.

## 4. Accounts

Each account type sees only the pages it needs. The sections below describe what
each account can do.

### 4.1 Admin / GCU Head

The **Admin** (Office / GCU Head) has the widest access and oversees Guidance and
Counseling plus system administration.

**Can do:**
- **Dashboard** with office-wide figures.
- **Students** — view the full student list, open a student's profile, **Add
  Student** (one at a time) or **Upload** a masterlist (Excel/CSV) with a preview
  that flags duplicates and same-name students before importing. Can **deactivate
  / reactivate** a student account (with a reason, e.g. Graduated, Transferred).
- **Referrals** — see the full referral queue, acknowledge, assign, and act on
  referrals; send **feedback slips** back to referrers.
- **Refer a Student / File a Complaint** — create referrals and complaints.
- **Cases** — open and manage counseling cases, record **session notes**,
  interventions and hand-offs between units.
- **Appointments** — schedule and manage appointments and call slips.
- **Reports** — office reports and statistics.
- **User Management** — create and manage **all** staff accounts and roles
  (GCU Staff, SDU Head, TMDU Head, TMDU Staff, Dean, Department Chair, Faculty),
  reset passwords, and activate/deactivate accounts (with a reason, kept in a
  history).
- **Audit Logs** — a complete record of who did what, for accountability.
- **Backup** — database backup records.
- **Management** — manage the reference data (see **System Admin**).

### 4.2 GCU Staff

Guidance and Counseling staff handle the day-to-day counseling workflow.

**Can do:**
- **Dashboard** for their caseload.
- **Students** — view profiles and student details.
- **Referrals** — work the referral queue, acknowledge and handle referrals, send
  feedback slips.
- **Refer a Student / File a Complaint** — create referrals and complaints.
- **Cases** — manage counseling cases, record session notes and interventions.
- **Appointments** — schedule counseling appointments and issue call slips.
- **Reports** — counseling reports.

GCU Staff do **not** manage other users' accounts.

### 4.3 SDU Head (Student Discipline Unit)

The SDU Head handles the disciplinary side.

**Can do:**
- **Dashboard** for discipline activity.
- **Complaints** — review and act on complaints filed against students.
- **Incident Reports** — review incident reports and track their status at each
  step.
- **Refer a Student / File a Complaint** and **Sent** lists.
- **Reports** — discipline reports.

### 4.4 TMDU Head

The TMDU Head oversees the Testing and Materials Development Unit and its staff.

**Can do:**
- **Dashboard** for the testing unit.
- **User Management (TMDU only)** — create and manage **TMDU Staff** accounts.
- **Refer a Student / File a Complaint** and **Sent** lists.
- Oversight of the testing workflow handled by TMDU Staff.

### 4.5 TMDU Staff

TMDU Staff run the psychological testing process.

**Can do:**
- **Dashboard** for testing.
- **Testing Records** — manage each testing record end to end: fee/official
  receipt, scheduling, administering tests, entering results, and sending the
  report back to the guidance unit.
- **Testing Appointments** — the testing calendar and queue.
- **Students / Referrals (shared view)** — open the student and referral details
  connected to a testing record.
- **Refer a Student / File a Complaint** and **Reports**.

### 4.6 Dean

A Dean oversees their **college** and its faculty.

**Can do:**
- **Dashboard** for their college.
- **Refer a Student** — refer students from their college and see what happened.
- **Sent Referrals / Complaints** — track what they have sent.
- **College Report** — how their college compares with others by number of
  referrals, plus a breakdown by status, service, program and month.
- **Faculty** — add and manage **Faculty** and **Department Chairs** within their
  college (one Department Chair per department). Upload a faculty masterlist for
  their college.
- **User Management (college scope)** — manage accounts within their own college.

### 4.7 Department Chair

A Department Chair manages faculty in **their own department**.

**Can do:**
- **Dashboard**.
- **Refer a Student** and **Sent** lists.
- **Faculty** — add and manage **Faculty** accounts within their department only
  (add one at a time or upload a masterlist, scoped to their department).

### 4.8 Faculty

Faculty members refer students and follow up on their referrals.

**Can do:**
- **Dashboard**.
- **Refer a Student** — file a referral for a student who needs guidance,
  discipline or testing support.
- **Sent Referrals / Complaints** — see the referrals/complaints they filed and
  the feedback the office sent back.
- **Notifications** and **My Account**.

Faculty do not see other students' cases or office-wide records — only what
relates to their own referrals.

### 4.9 System Admin

The System Admin maintains the reference data that the whole system relies on.

**Can do:**
- **Management** — maintain the master lists used across iCARE:
  - **Colleges**, **Departments** and **Programs**
  - **Academic Years** (with semester and mid-year dates)
  - **Form Options** (the dropdown choices on referral and other forms)
  - **Document Settings** (control numbers, revision and effectivity printed on
    official slips and forms)
  Items can be archived instead of deleted, and deletions are recorded with a
  reason.
- **Refer a Student / File a Complaint** (if needed).

> **Note:** the **Admin / GCU Head** can also reach **Management**, so in a small
> office one person may handle both.

### 4.10 Student

Students use the **Student Portal** to take part in their own care.

**Can do:**
- **Dashboard** — a summary of their referrals and appointments.
- **My Referrals** — the status of each referral made about them, with
  easy-to-read status badges.
- **Appointments** — view appointments, **pick a schedule** offered to them,
  confirm or reschedule, and see the details.
- **File a Complaint** — report an incident (with the required attachments /
  affidavit where applicable).
- **Sent Referrals / Complaints** — what the student has sent.
- **Testing** — their psychological testing schedule and status (when referred for
  testing).
- **My Account** — update their details and password.
- **Notifications** — alerts when something about their request changes.

> Self-referral (referring yourself) may be turned on or off by the office.

## 5. Common tasks, step by step

### Refer a student (Faculty, Dean, Chair, OSS staff)
1. Sign in and open **Refer a Student**.
2. Choose the student (search by Student ID or name).
3. Pick the **type of referral** and describe the **nature of concern**; set the
   **urgency**.
4. Add any attachments, then **Submit**. A referral code is generated and the
   office is notified.
5. Track it under **Sent Referrals**; when the office acts, you receive a
   **feedback slip**.

### Acknowledge and handle a referral (GCU/OSS staff)
1. Open **Referrals** — new ones appear automatically.
2. Open a referral, **Acknowledge** it, and **assign** it (to yourself or a
   colleague).
3. Open or link a **case**, schedule an **appointment**, and record **session
   notes** as you go.
4. When finished, update the status and **send feedback** to the referrer.

### File a complaint (students, staff, faculty)
1. Open **File a Complaint**.
2. Fill in who the complaint is about, the **violation type**, the **description**
   and **incident date**.
3. Attach the required evidence / affidavit, agree to the certification, and
   **Submit**. The SDU reviews it.

### Schedule an appointment (OSS staff) / confirm one (student)
- **Staff:** from a case or referral, create an appointment (date, time, type,
  location) and issue a call slip. The student is notified.
- **Student:** open **Appointments**, pick an offered schedule, and **confirm** or
  **reschedule**.

### Add or upload students (Admin / GCU Head)
- **Add Student** — fill the form for one student.
- **Upload** — choose an Excel/CSV masterlist. Review the **preview** (it flags
  duplicate IDs and same-name students) before confirming the import.

### Manage accounts (Admin, Dean, Department Chair, TMDU Head)
- Open **User Management** (staff) or **Faculty** (Dean/Chair).
- **Add** an account (choose the role where allowed), or **upload** a list.
- Reset a password, or **deactivate/reactivate** an account with a reason.

## 6. Troubleshooting & support

**I can't sign in.**
- Check you used the right card (Faculty / OSS Personnel / Student) and the right
  username (email for staff, Student ID for students).
- Passwords are case-sensitive. Use **Forgot password?** if unsure.
- After several wrong attempts, sign-in locks for a few minutes — wait and try
  again.

**The first page load is slow.**
- Give it a moment; the system wakes up and then runs normally.

**I changed something but still see the old version.**
- Do a **hard refresh**: `Ctrl` + `Shift` + `R` (Windows) or `Cmd` + `Shift` + `R`
  (Mac). This loads the latest version instead of the cached one.

**My new referral/case isn't showing on someone else's screen.**
- The main lists refresh on their own every few seconds. If needed, reload the
  page.

**I didn't get the password reset / notification email.**
- Check your **spam** folder, and use **Resend email**.

**Need help?**
- Contact the **Office of Student Services** (the contact details are on the
  public home page, under *Contact us*).
